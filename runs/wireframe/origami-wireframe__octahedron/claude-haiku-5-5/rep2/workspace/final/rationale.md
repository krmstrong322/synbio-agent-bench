Octahedron with 35 nm edges (vertices at ±24.75 nm on the axes). Each of the 12 edges is a DX pair of two 84-bp helices, one group per edge, placed at the edge midpoint with the pair's midline on the edge line (rows 2.6 nm apart, group axis along the edge). Helix ends sit ~3.2 nm short of each vertex. Scaffold: a single linear strand of M13mp18 (rotation 5587, 2085 nt) that traverses all 24 helices end-to-end, with 3-nt loopouts at the 23 vertex hops; the hop matching and edge orientations were chosen by search for a single cycle with minimal total hop length (max hop ~5 nm). Each DX pair has antiparallel scaffold; staple crossovers within each pair at the valid offsets (spacing 21 bp, [27,48,69] or [22,43,64] depending on role), with U-shaped end staples and 42-nt rings nicked mid-way. 96.7% of scaffold is paired; staples 30-54 nt. No vertex staples were added, so vertex connectivity relies on the scaffold hops.
