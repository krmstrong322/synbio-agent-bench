Wireframe octahedron: 12 edges (35 nm vertex-to-vertex, octahedron vertices at ±24.75 nm on the axes), each edge a group of two parallel 84-bp helices (offsets 1-84, row offset ±1.3 nm along n x t). Group orientation from yaw/pitch/roll of the edge direction. Each DX pair uses full staple crossovers at offsets 22, 43, 64 (valid for the pair per find_crossover_offsets); staples are 42 nt (two-domain end U-staples and ring staples with one nick each). Single scaffold (2103 nt, M13 from rotation 5587) runs each helix once in an Eulerian circuit over the vertices (antiparallel helices per edge), joined at each vertex by 1-2.6 nm hops with 3-8 nt loopouts; the circuit was chosen to minimise hop lengths and opened at the longest hop. check_design passes; 95.9% scaffold paired; all staples 42 nt.
