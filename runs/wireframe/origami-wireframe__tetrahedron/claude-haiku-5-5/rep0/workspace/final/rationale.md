Wireframe tetrahedron, 35 nm edges. Six DX edges, each a group of two 84-bp helices (28.6 nm) centred on the edge line, rows offset ±1.3 nm along n×t (n outward normal). Groups oriented with forward = edge direction, yaw/pitch/roll from group_angles. Scaffold: one 1057-nt contiguous M13 fragment routed as a directed Eulerian circuit of the doubled tetrahedron (12 helix traversals, 11 vertex hops; the longest hop is left open as the scaffold's free ends). Each vertex hop is a 3-7 nt loopout between helix ends 1.9-4.5 nm apart. Antiparallel scaffold on the two helices of each edge. Staples: hairpin staples (21-27 bp per helix, one staple crossover per boundary, placed at offsets validated by find_crossover_offsets), plus 36-nt / 22-nt single-domain end staples. Unpaired scaffold = 49 loop nt (~95.4% paired). Open issues: vertices are held only by the scaffold hops (no vertex staples); staple nicks are not staggered.
