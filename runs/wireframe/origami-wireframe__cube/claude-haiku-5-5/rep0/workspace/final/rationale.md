Scaffold is a single 2155-nt fragment of M13mp18 routed as an Euler circuit of the bidirected cube graph: each edge's two helices run antiparallel, and consecutive helices hop at shared vertices through 2-11 nt unpaired loops (139 nt total, ~93% paired). Staples: per edge, two DX crossover sets at boundaries 22, 43, 64 bp form 42-nt nicked rings, plus 44- and 40-nt end staples (48 staples, 40-44 nt). Scaffold fully covered on all helices.
