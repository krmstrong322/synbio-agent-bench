8 helices on a square lattice in one row (grid (i,0)), each spanning offsets 0-96 (96 bp). Scaffold is one 768-nt M13mp18 fragment (rotation 5587): helix 0 forward, then a raster 1 reverse, 2 forward ... 7 reverse, with turns at the helix ends (offset 95 top, 0 bottom). Staples are full-length per helix, joined by full crossovers at 16-bp alternating sides: pairs (even,odd) at offsets 9, 41, 73 and (odd,even) at 25, 57; then nicked so every staple is 18-46 nt (4 x 18, 16 x 32, 4 x 46). All 768 scaffold bases are paired; 43 crossovers total, every adjacent helix pair is connected along its length (gaps at helix ends). Known weak points: the scaffold turns and some staple crossovers are not at geometrically optimal offsets (the valid-offset lists could not give 90+ bp helices with both turns on valid positions), and the top/bottom ends of the odd-even interfaces have few crossovers.
