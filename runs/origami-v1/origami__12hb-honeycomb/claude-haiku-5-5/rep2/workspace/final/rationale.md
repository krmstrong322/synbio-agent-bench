12 helices on the honeycomb lattice in a compact 3-column x 4-row block (cells (2,-1..2), (3,-1..2), (4,-1..2)); every helix has 2-3 neighbours (the block is two fused hexagonal rings). Helix lengths are 80-87 bp. The single scaffold is a 1000-nt contiguous M13mp18 fragment that snakes through all 12 helices (Hamiltonian path 1,0,4,8,9,5,6,2,3,7,11,10), alternating direction along the path, with half-crossover turns at helix ends at offsets chosen from find_crossover_offsets. Staple crossovers are full crossovers at valid offsets (every ~21 bp per adjacent pair; residues 6/13/20 mod 21 keep crossover sites on a helix >=7 bp apart), and nicks are placed to give 30-55 nt staples, each with a domain of at least 14 bp in most cases. All scaffold bases are covered by staples (100%). Open issue: some staples are built from 7-bp domains, so they may be less stable.
